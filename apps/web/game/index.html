Controls & building

WASD / arrows: move · Space: jump · Shift: sprint · Q/E or right-drag: rotate · Scroll: zoom · 1/2/3: tools · 4: wave · R: respawn.

Choose Build, then click the ground or the face of a block. Choose Remove to remove your own blocks. You can build within 5 blocks of your character. Checkpoints and original scenery stay protected.

On touchscreens, use the arrows and Jump. Drag the world in Explore mode to look around; tap with Build or Remove selected to edit.
